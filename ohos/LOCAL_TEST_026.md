# 0.26 local keyboard lifecycle check

Use a user-owned compatible device after signing the unsigned HAP with the official SDK. The0.25 approved interface,52vp Stop, single video Surface, output Connect/Arm and settings drafts are unchanged. Continuous Drive and general gamepad control are not in this release.

1. In Settings→Input choose a harmless visible action (for example show library) for a key hold. Keep output disconnected/disarmed. Hold the key, then move focus to another app/window before its delay. Returning must not trigger it; release the key, then press again to execute normally. If the OS did not deliver the release outside the app, one complete press/release after returning may be needed to clear the held-key guard.
2. Test phone/tablet external keyboard and PC/freeform windows, with the app visible but inactive. Settings→Input should report suspended shortcuts. Existing video may keep playing on ordinary focus loss. App background retains its stronger existing safety policy.
3. Hold during Home/folding/system interruption and before/after rotation. No Release/Click action should be generated by cancellation. A new press after release must work. API20 may not emit native key CANCEL; window/background cancellation must still work. Record device model/OS/API and exact steps if an event is not delivered.
4. Focus a number/text editor and use an IME. Ordinary typing must not execute shortcuts or unexpectedly confirm drafts. F12 must stop immediately when the focused app actually receives it; it is not promised as an OS-global key. Touch Stop remains available without a keyboard.
5. Replace/reopen the page or close/recreate its Ability while a harmless shortcut has pending follow-up actions. Old actions must not run in the new page or clear its focus. No output reconnect or Arm may happen by restoration.
6. If “无法确认主窗口焦点” appears, shortcuts stay closed. Reopen the app window and report the device/OS and exact lifecycle steps. Do not bypass the guard or test with moving hardware first.

This checklist still requires device execution. Mock, source inspection and SDK compilation alone are not evidence of actual focus/IME/keyboard delivery or visual acceptance. Continue LOCAL_TEST_025 for appearance and LOCAL_TEST_023 for narrow experimental serial scope.
